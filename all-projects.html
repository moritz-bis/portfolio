leather net

tweaking the properties of leather

2025
in development
Obertshausen (DE)
harolds-bags.com

« protection and efficency
Laptops need protection just like mangos. This project is an aesthetic proposal as much as it is a way to process imperfect leather.

« prototyping
Many manual prototypes were needed, to fine tune the pattern and to come up with a economic way of manufacturing it.

« Scraps are small
Many leather pieces can't be used, becasue they are too small or too textured for use in leather bags. This method of cutting the leather however, drastically enlarges the pieces and covers almost all imperfections.

« inspiration
The idea was to take the radically optimized structure of industrial packaging but bring it into the warm context of leather.

humus

bio-waste isn't waste

2024
HfG Offenbach (DE)

« bio-waste as a resource
"humus" shifts the focus away from waste towards reuse and emphasises the circularity of organic waste. The composting eliminates unpleasant smells, meaning "humus" can be positioned almost anywhere.

« technicalities
Finished humus can be harvested through a grid at the bottom, while sensors monitor the composting, communicating any problems. The system of "continuous flow-through vermi-composting" is also used industrially.

« a greener system
The composters are monitored and connetced through sensors, creating a decentralised network. Composting on site and only transporting the resulting compost substantially reduces the enviromental impact.

« organic waste as a problem
Bio-waste is a valuable resource e.g. as organic fertiliser. But today’s recycling systems require extensive transportation, which makes the system unprofitable and enviromentally problematic.

« form and material
Changing the image of bio-waste means redesigning the waste container. To break the garbage bin association, material and form research was oriented on world of plants.

V-bin

simplified zero waste

2021
HfG Offenbach (DE)

« a clean dispenser
V-bin is designed to be the simplest way to offer zero waste shopping. Reduced maintenance means lower cost, and fewer arguments against it.

« iris valve
An iris valve clamps shut or releases 5 to 20 kg bags. The design of the handle allows for easy placment of a bag while eliminating direct contact.

« looking at the system
V-bin not only reduces maintenace cost but also simplifies transitioning to zero waste. The design can take any large bag format, including the wholesale bags most manufacturers already offer.

« the status quo
Even though you are technically buying less, the reality is: zero waste shopping is expensive. A problem that arises mainly thanks to high maintenace costs.

guidance and niches

2019
HfG Offenbach (DE)
together with L. Handon

Like a dynamic wall, "paravent" can adapt to the various needs of public spaces. Each module can turn around its vertical axis, creating niches, passages or guidance.

Humans like to wait in calm niches. At the same time they like a smooth flowing crowd. Sometimes these things have to happen in the same place, juts at different times.

Research and on-site surveys at Frankfurts central station clearly showed how dynamic public spaces are. A good example is the difference between noon and rush hour at a train station.

microplaza

multilayered urban design

2022
HfG Offenbach (DE)
together with D. Bösterling and P. Witkowski

« topographic urban design
Buy using a topography based upon a generative layout micorplaza creates a highly functional yet pleasant street layout.

« parametric generation
The layout is generated parametrically, using the house entrances and a fixed street width of 3m for emergency traffic. The resulting islands are then assigned different functions.

« three levels
Three levels are created: elevated islands as social space, a sidewalk at ground level and lowered pools of greenery. The elevation not only guides people but also steers rainwater towards open ground.

« superblocks for Offenbach
Microplaza iterates on the concept of superblocks, creating a flexible framework for any city or street.
